Upload project gallery screenshots here.

Recommended filenames for automatic loading:

- dashnexus-01.png through dashnexus-06.png
- society-01.png through society-09.png
- travel-01.png through travel-05.png

Subfolders are also supported:

- dashnexus/01.png through dashnexus/06.png
- society/01.png through society/09.png
- travel/01.png through travel/05.png

Generic fallback names are also supported:

- screenshot-01.png, screen-01.png, or 01.png

The current root image upload names are also supported:

- image/T-1 through image/T-6 for DashNexus
- image/T-7 through image/T-15 for Society Management
- image/T-16 through image/T-20 for Tour & Travel if used later

The gallery tries PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP formats automatically.
